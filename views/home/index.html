About Us

We Leave A Delicious Memory For You

Kafe Affair is a charming sea-facing café in Daman, where good food meets calming ocean views. Designed for relaxed moments and meaningful conversations, it’s the perfect place to spend quality time over comforting coffee, artisanal pizzas, gourmet burgers, and signature snacks.
