About Kafe Affair

Crafting delicious food, specialty coffee, and unforgettable sea-facing memories in Daman.

A Place for Coffee & Ocean Views

Kafe Affair was founded with a simple vision — to create a warm, cozy sanctuary where people can enjoy quality coffee, mouth-watering gourmet bites, and meaningful conversations.

Situated right along the serene coastline of Daman, every detail of our café is curated to evoke comfort. From artisanal roasted beans to freshly toasted wraps and wood-fired style pizzas, every dish tells a story of passion.
